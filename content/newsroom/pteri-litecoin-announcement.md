## Migration decision: archive

The original announcement, posted to X on 15 July 2025, introduced PTERI as an identity and
authorization layer **for Litecoin**.

It is archived rather than republished for four reasons:

1. **Obsolete positioning.** The handoff explicitly rules out automatically republishing
   Web3-first framing. KAKR's current positioning is identity and authorization security
   for people, AI agents and enterprise systems — Litecoin is not the frame.
2. **X was the only destination.** There is no article body to migrate; the record consists
   of a card pointing at a post on X.
3. **Miscategorised.** It was stored as a product update with no availability label, which
   the current schema does not permit.
4. **Unstable imagery.** Its hero image was hot-linked from `pbs.twimg.com`, outside
   KAKR's control.
