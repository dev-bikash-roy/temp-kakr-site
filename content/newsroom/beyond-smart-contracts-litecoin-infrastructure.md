## Migration decision: archive

Posted to X on 20 July 2025, this piece argued for Litecoin's proof-of-work security as a
foundation for identity and authorization infrastructure rather than for token speculation.

It is archived rather than republished because:

1. **Blockchain-first framing.** The argument's premise is that the blockchain layer is the
   product story. KAKR's current story is identity and authorization for people, AI agents
   and enterprise systems.
2. **X was the only destination.** There is no article body to migrate.
3. **Unstable imagery.** The hero image was hot-linked from `pbs.twimg.com`.

The underlying technical position is not wrong — it is simply no longer the lede, and
republishing it as current engineering commentary would misrepresent where the product is.
